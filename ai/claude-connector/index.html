    Use theprototype from claude.ai and voice

    Add theprototype as a connector in claude.ai, press Connect Claude in your open theprototype tab, and talk Claude through building — in a chat on the web or desktop, in the Claude phone app, or out loud in voice mode. Claude works in your own tab, with your own access: everything it does shows up for everyone in your session and can be undone, exactly like your own edits.

    New

    The Claude connector needs a theprototype account and works on theprototype.app (the hosted app). Running the open-source app yourself? Use the agent bridge instead — it gives Claude Code or Claude Desktop the same tools over a session link.

    What you need

    • A theprototype account, signed in on theprototype.app.
    • A Claude plan that lets you add custom connectors in claude.ai.
    • A theprototype tab that stays open while Claude works (a computer is easiest; your phone can be the one you talk to).

    Set it up (once)

    1. Press Add to Claude in the AI assistant window (shown while no AI provider is set up), or find theprototype in Claude's connector directory. Or add it by hand: in claude.ai open Settings ▸ Connectors ▸ Add custom connector and enter https://theprototype.app/mcp; the window has a copy button beside the address.
    2. claude.ai sends you to theprototype to approve it. The page names the app asking and where it will return (claude.ai). Leave Allow changes ticked to let Claude build, or untick it for a look-only connection that can only read the scene and take pictures. Press Allow. Not signed in? Use Sign in on theprototype.app, then I signed in — retry.
    3. Back in claude.ai, switch the connector on for your chat (the tools menu under the message box).

    Connect your tab (each time you build)

    1. Open theprototype.app, sign in, and open or join the scene you want to work on.
    2. Press the AI button and press Connect Claude (also in Settings ▸ Connected apps). A chip reads Claude connected · this tab. The AI button always opens the window; signed out, the same card offers Sign in to connect, which opens your profile menu at the top right, the one place to sign in. With no AI provider set up, the window also offers Use your own AI provider (Open settings). Working with a local model or your own key while signed out? Sign in to connect stays in the window's header.
    3. Ask Claude something: "Build a campfire with five logs and a warm light", "Make the flame flicker", "What can you do here?"
    1. Sign in to connect opens your profile menu: sign in first.
    2. Add to Claude adds the connector in claude.ai — once only.
    3. How it works opens this page.

    One tab at a time: pressing Connect Claude in a second tab asks first, then moves the connection there. If your network blips, the tab reconnects by itself.

    Keep the tab open — and in front

    Claude's tools run in that tab. If you close it, Claude answers "No theprototype tab is connected — open theprototype.app, sign in and press Connect Claude". A tab left in the background is slowed down by the browser and cannot take pictures; after a few minutes the tab warns you. On a phone, talk to Claude in the Claude app and keep theprototype open on a computer or tablet beside you.

    Voice mode

    In voice mode Claude keeps its answers short and says what changed in plain words ("Added three trees by the fire") — it never reads out ids or numbers. Good things to say:

    • "What can you do here?" — a short spoken summary of what it can build in this tab.
    • "Put a table in the middle and four chairs around it."
    • "Make the box spin slowly", "Open the door when I click it."
    • "Make it bigger", "Paint the walls light blue", "Undo that" (or press Ctrl+Z yourself).

    What Claude can and cannot do

    Claude can Claude cannot
    Add shapes, lights and groups; move, resize, recolour, rename, hide and delete them Open files or import them from your computer, or change your settings
    Make things move and react with flow nodes (spin, bounce, follow a path, open on click) and look up how each node wires Install modules, approve people who knock, or answer the app's questions for you
    Add physics and run the simulation in your tab at its full frame rate, whether you host or joined (your own assistant's Physics tools setting is not changed) See anything outside the theprototype tab, or move your camera
    Animate a model's named parts, and see where everyone stands and looks, so "put a tree in front of me" lands in front of you, in VR too (a look-only connection sees only your own position)
    Ask your tab to join a session from a link you give it: nothing happens until you press Join on the card in the tab (see below)
    Write small code behaviours Do more than you can: a viewer's Claude can look but not build, and locked objects stay locked
    Take a picture of the scene to check its work, and save the open scene as a new version in your Library (see below) Keep working after you close the tab
    Make shapes the basic objects cannot — a vase on a lathe, a rail along a path, an extruded sign, a mesh from its own points — and reshape or model them (Shapes and modelling) Fetch an image from your computer or your local network
    Change materials and textures, node shaders, UV mapping and the scene's post effects; a change to the whole scene's look asks you first with Allow / Deny in the tab (Materials and looks)
    Set the point a thing turns about, and put an image into your library from an https address or one it draws (Undo, pivots and images)

    Joining a session from a link

    Give Claude a session or invite link and ask it to join. Your tab shows Join a session?, naming the session and who asks (Claude (connector)). Cancel, or 90 seconds with no answer, refuses. After Join the app's usual questions still apply: leaving your current session, another signaling server, the host's Approve, and what to do with work you have open. One question at a time: if another is open in the tab, Claude is told to try later. A look-only connection cannot ask to join.

    Claude only says it joined once the connection is really up. If the session cannot be reached, the host refuses or you cancel, it tells you what happened, and status shows the last result and whether the host is still deciding.

    Saving the scene

    "Save this" is the same save as Ctrl+S: a new version, and Version history can bring back older ones. In someone else's session it saves on the host. A viewer cannot save, a private scene cannot be saved this way, and Claude cannot write over a different scene: if the name it picks is taken, it is told to choose another or to ask you to open that scene first.

    Importing files

    Since 1.61 Claude can bring in a 3D model with create_shape import: a glTF, GLB or OBJ it sends in parts, or one at a public https address. It goes into your library first, and its named parts can be animated (details). Other files, and files on your computer, you drop into the tab yourself; then ask Claude to place or change them.

    Scripts, spin and particles

    • Scripts run every frame in a sandbox, on a view of their object, starting again from the base pose. Write the pose from the time, as in object.rotation.y = base.rot[1] + elapsed * speed. elapsed is seconds of the game round, the same for everyone inside a round; outside a round it counts from your own Play press. time is unchanged. Something like += 0.01 will not build up: the warning says why and points at elapsed. Errors from running scripts appear in status.
    • Animated objects: on an object that is being animated, Claude reads and edits the pose you authored, and is told it is animated and where it is right now.
    • Physics in a group: the simulation only runs objects at the top of the scene. If an object with physics is inside a group, Claude is told so and can move it out, or set physics on the group itself.
    • Particles on a rotated parent: in world space, an emitter can follow the object (a rocket's exhaust) or keep world axes (a fire that burns straight up). Gravity is acceleration along world Y in m/s²: negative falls, positive rises.

    See and undo what it did

    Every step Claude takes is a line labelled Claude (connector) in Agent activity, with its own Undo. Anyone in the session sees the changes as they happen, and anyone with edit access can undo them from their own screen.

    Or just say "undo that": Claude takes back its own last step. If someone has changed the same things since, it changes nothing and tells you who changed what.

    Screenshots have a 4:3 shape whatever your window's shape, except the current view, which keeps your canvas. Claude can pick the angle (front, back, left, right, top, below, iso, or a yaw and pitch), a camera position and target, the objects to frame, a zoom (0.1-10) and a margin (0.5-4). It is told which camera it used, and your own view never moves.

    Tool families

    To keep its list short, Claude on claude.ai reaches the newer tools in families: one tool with an action. They do exactly what the agent tools of the same job do, with the same limits:

    family actions the agent tools behind them
    create_shape lathe, tube, extrude, mesh, import, text, path create_lathe, create_tube, extrude_shape, create_mesh, import_shape, create_text (Shapes and modelling), create_path (Paths)
    edit_shape params, mesh, update, duplicate, pivot, pivot_group set_geometry, edit_mesh, update_mesh, duplicate, set_pivot, create_pivot
    set_look material, list_materials, get_material, shader, edit_shader, assign_shader, shader_nodes, post, get_post, post_graph, unwrap, uv, quality, sky, light, environment set_material … set_uv, set_quality (Materials and looks); set_sky, set_light, get_environment (HUD, sky and play)
    hud create, list, update, panel create_hud, list_hud, edit_hud, hud_panel (HUD, sky and play)
    game_setup spawn, play_camera, levels, portal, checkpoint, loading place_spawn, set_play_camera, set_levels, set_portal, set_checkpoint, set_loading (HUD, sky and play)
    assets search, place, prefab, image search_assets, place_asset, prefab (Worlds), add_image (Undo, pivots and images)
    effects terrain, scatter, water, particles, sound, music terrain, scatter, water, create_particles, place_sound, set_music (Worlds with an agent)
    run inspect, validate, logs inspect_runtime, validate_scene, logs (Test and fix a game)

    undo_last is a tool of its own. A look-only connection can still use the actions that only read (list_materials, get_material, shader_nodes, get_post, environment, hud list, assets search and all of run). playtest (a test player) runs only on an agent's own page, so it is not on claude.ai.

    New in 1.59 — the same tools, with more in them:

    • create_shape takes {"shapes": [{"action": "lathe", "lathe": {…}}, …]} with no action: up to 50 shapes in one call and one undo step, checked first, so nothing is made if one entry is wrong (the error names it, shapes[3]).
    • A shape made with parentUuid is placed in the group's own space — its position, rotation, scale and its points — and with no position it sits at the group's origin. mesh also takes colors, materials + faceMaterials and flatShading (Shapes and modelling).
    • create_objects makes copies of an object (from, count, offset, randomRot, randomScale, seed), and create_objects / update_objects take lookAt and castShadow / receiveShadow (details).
    • edit_shape pivot_group takes local; list_scene gives each mesh's vertex and triangle count and its bounds; status says how heavy the scene is (sceneBudget); set_look names the material presets.

    New in 1.60 — four families, so Claude can build a small game and check it:

    • hud makes the game's screen overlay: timers, scores, bars, buttons, images and panels (HUD, sky and play).
    • game_setup says where Play starts, turns on first-person walking or flying, and sets the view a scene opens with.
    • set_look sky, light and environment change the sky, fog, exposure, the sun and the ambient light. They change everyone's view, so your tab asks the host first.
    • run tells Claude what the game does: validate lists what would not work when someone presses Play, each with its fix; inspect says why something did not fire; logs reads the tab's recent errors (Test and fix a game).
    • where_are_users takes pings, and create_objects / update_objects take at: {ping}, snap: "floor", align and face_user: point at a spot and say "put a lamp here" (Here, this and you).

    Worlds (Worlds with an agent):

    • effects builds places: terrain with hills and paint, scatter (a forest, rocks), water, particles (fireflies are new), positional sounds and the background music.
    • assets search finds models, sounds, images and CC0 materials. place and prefab put them in the scene.
    • set_look material takes pattern (wood, brick, marble …). create_shape mesh takes subdivide: 1-2, and edit_shape update sends a mesh new vertices.

    New in 1.64 — no new tool names, more in the families and the nodes:

    • game_setup levels, portal, checkpoint and loading build a game of several levels: the one Levels table, doorways, respawn pads and the loading screen (Levels and progress, reference).
    • create_shape path makes a Path with waits, speeds and events per point, and can set objects walking it (walkers, spread by offset).
    • create_flow_nodes knows the new nodes: On Hover, Hover Animate, Toggle Animation, On Play start / stop, On Path Event, and the room nodes of a furnished room (Oscillate, Move / Rotate / Scale To, Light Switch, Wind Sway, Flowing Water, Ambient Animal, Grabbable, Highlight, Press Button, Sit Spot). Everyday words find them (door, lamp, cat, fish, seat, button, hover, wind, river, pickup), and describe_node_types documents every input and setting.
    • run inspect shows the game kit's round, score and pickups; a kit action that cannot run is refused with its reason, and a behaviour handler that throws is rolled back (rolledBack: true).

    New in 1.63 — no new tool names, more in the families:

    • set_look preset applies a pro look (product, cinematic, stylised) in one Undo step.
    • set_look bake bakes corner shadows and bounced light (bake: {do, quality, wait, light, ao}).
    • run photo renders a path-traced photo of your view; a PC in the session renders it for a headset.

    New in 1.61 — no new tool names, more in the families:

    • create_shape text writes 3D text, flat or solid with a bevel; edit_shape params changes its words and style, and assets fonts lists the fonts Claude may use (the bundled ones and those in your Library).
    • edit_shape duplicate copies an object in a row, or makes its mirror image (a left arm becomes the right one).
    • create_shape import brings in a glTF, GLB or OBJ model; mesh colours are sRGB with #rrggbb allowed.
    • effects scatter takes source (real copies of something Claude built) and avoid (keep paths clear).
    • set_look quality asks you, on your own device, for higher quality when a phone hides the post effects; post says whether your screen draws them, and screenshot takes postEffects: true.
    • status's sceneBudget names the heaviest objects (top) and says whether the frame time is just the screen's refresh (vsync).
    • A parentUuid that names nothing is an error and makes nothing; groups stay groups after a restore.
    • assets upload sends a sound or an HDR sky as bytes (Claude's own music, or a file it fetched itself) and answers with its hash. assets search lists the sounds of the sound packs, and effects sound / music take them.
    • set_look sky takes a Poly Haven HDRI from search.
    • effects terrain: height is the highest peak, layers show at once, and paintBy: "height" paints them low to high. scatter keeps circles clear (avoid / rules.avoid with a radius) and takes an id.
    • front tells lookAt and face_user which way a model faces.
    • screenshot: view: "camera" looks through a scene camera, hud: true adds the game HUD, and people are left out unless avatars: true.
    • Pressing Allow lets the change through on the first repeat; the answer says pending, allowed, denied or expired. Pings mark the spot you clicked, and wait_for_edit wakes when you ping.
    • list_scene near / bounds take by: "touches" or "inside"; sceneBudget has callsBySource and never calls a frame on the screen's refresh slow.
    • Claude keeps your names and ids as they are: object names, prefab ids and undo lines are not rewritten.

    Claude reads its tool list when it connects, so it sees these after the connector reconnects.

    Stop it

    • Disconnect (next to the chip, or in Settings ▸ Connected apps) stops Claude at once, including a step it is in the middle of. Press Connect Claude again to continue.
    • Revoke in Settings ▸ Connected apps removes the connector's access to your account completely; claude.ai has to be approved again before it can work in any tab. You can also remove the connector in claude.ai's settings.

    Privacy and limits

    • Nothing runs on our servers except a relay: Claude's request goes to your tab, your tab does the work, and the answer goes back. Your scene is never stored by the connector, and we do not log what Claude asks or what your tab answers — only counts (how many calls, how long they took).
    • Pictures come from the theprototype canvas only, downscaled, and never while the tab is hidden.
    • If the link to your tab drops for a moment, Claude's call carries on and answers once the link is back. If something can't run right now, such as a tool whose code failed to load, status tells Claude, usually with the fix: reload the tab.
    • There is a fair-use limit on calls per minute. If Claude goes too fast it is told to wait a few seconds; ask again and it continues.
